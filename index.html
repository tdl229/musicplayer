MeloPlay: Offline Music Player

Import local lossless audio files, extract high-quality M4A audio from videos, and customize your sound 100% offline with a 10-band studio equalizer.

Key Features

Offline Music & Wi-Fi Transfer

Import MP3, M4A, WAV, and FLAC files with embedded cover art from Files / iCloud Drive or transfer songs wirelessly from your Mac or PC browser over local Wi-Fi.

Video to Audio Extractor & Playlists

Extract crisp M4A audio tracks directly from local videos in your Photos library or Files app on-device, and organize your favorite songs into custom offline playlists.

10-Band Equalizer & Bass Boost

Fine-tune 31Hz–16kHz frequencies with 22 studio presets, dedicated Bass Booster, up to 200% Volume Booster, variable playback speed, and Sleep Timer.

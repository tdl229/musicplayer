C. DATA SETS WE COLLECT AND FOR WHAT PURPOSE:

MeloPlay: Offline Music Player is designed with a privacy-first, local-storage architecture. We do not require you to register an account, and we do not operate backend servers that collect or store your personal identity or your local audio files.

Non-Personal Data

During your interaction with the App, we may process non-identifiable technical and preference information locally on your device (“Non-Personal Data”), such as your selected app language, playback speed, equalizer settings, theme preferences, device model, and operating system version.

Personal Data & Device Permissions

We do not knowingly collect or process any Special Categories of Personal Data (such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, genetic data, biometric data, or health data). Below is a detailed breakdown of the data and device permissions involved when you use the App:

DATA SET / PERMISSION PURPOSE AND OPERATIONS LAWFUL BASIS & STORAGE
Local Audio Files & Media Library
(MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC imported via Files, iCloud Drive, or Wi-Fi Transfer)		 When you import audio files into the App, the App reads the audio file data and embedded metadata (title, artist, album, duration, cover artwork) solely to organize your offline music library, create local playlists, and play audio through the built-in equalizer and player. Processed solely on your device based on your explicit action and consent. We never upload, transmit, or store your local audio files on any external server.
Video-to-Audio Extraction
(User-selected videos via iOS Photo Picker / Document Picker)		 When you use “Extract Audio from Video”, the App reads only the specific video files you explicitly select from Photos or Files to extract an offline M4A audio track and capture a thumbnail cover image locally on your device. Local Network Permission
(NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription / Wi-Fi Browser Transfer)		 When you open the “Wi-Fi Transfer” page in the App, the App starts a temporary local HTTP server on your iPhone/iPad over your local Wi-Fi network so you can upload audio files directly from a Mac or PC browser on the same Wi-Fi network. Processed strictly over your local area network (LAN) upon your explicit activation of the Wi-Fi Transfer feature. No audio files or network traffic pass through our servers. You may disable Local Network permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Processed to perform our contract with you. We do not collect, access, or store your credit card numbers, Apple ID credentials, or billing address.
